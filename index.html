Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone, and in your own iPhone backups if you use them. There is no account and no server.

How do I move or keep my data? Settings → Export all data saves a file you control.

How do I delete everything? Settings → Delete all data, or delete the app.

Speaking a note does not work. On-device speech recognition must be available for your language. Check Settings → Permissions in the app, or type your note instead.
