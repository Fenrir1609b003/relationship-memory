Relationship Memory Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026

Summary

Relationship Memory does not collect, transmit, sell or share your data. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

The people you add, the notes you type, speak or photograph about them, and the facts extracted from those notes. Photos and audio themselves are never stored; only the text read from them is. This is stored in one file inside the app's private storage, encrypted by iOS Data Protection whenever your iPhone is locked. It is included in your own iPhone backups (iCloud or computer) if you have backups enabled.

On-device processing

Notes and the questions you ask are analysed on your iPhone, using Apple Intelligence where available. Speech uses Apple's on-device recognition only; if it is unavailable, speaking a note is disabled rather than sent to a server. Text in photos is read on your iPhone.

Permissions

Microphone and speech recognition: used only while you speak a note.

Camera: used only when you photograph a business card or badge. Photos you choose from your library are picked through Apple's photo picker, so the app sees only the photo you select.

Location: used only when you tap the current-location button. The app requests approximate location and asks Apple's geocoding service for a place name such as a city. Only that place name is saved.

Contacts: if you allow it, the app reads your Contacts on your iPhone to recognise people you mention, fill in their details and point out duplicates. Your Contacts are never uploaded, and the app never changes them. Without this permission you can still pick individual people with Apple's contact picker.

Face ID: used only to unlock the app if you turn on app lock. The app never sees your biometric data.

People you write about

You are responsible for the information you record about other people and for respecting the laws that apply to you. The app is designed for your own recap after a conversation, not for recording other people.

Your control

Settings lets you export all your data as a file and permanently delete all data. Deleting the app also deletes all its data from the iPhone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the app and on its App Store page. For questions, use the support link on the app's App Store page.
